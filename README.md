**Midnight Ramen** — a one-cook noodle counter that opens when the last train has gone. Three seats, three tickets, and one bowl on the pass: every customer prints the exact order of their bowl on their ticket, so the cook reads the board, picks the crate that comes next, and builds it step by step before the patience bar empties. Bowls pay their menu price plus a tip that shrinks with every second the customer waits; a wrong crate eats patience, and three walkouts close the shop. Canvas 2D, no library, no network, no build step — open `game/index.html` and play.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
